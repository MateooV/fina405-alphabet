FX risk · issuing in EUR

Euro moves change the USD value of the bond

Medium

The euro floats freely against the dollar. A 10% euro appreciation raises the USD value of the liability by +11.11% (Section 5). Alphabet is a net receiver of EUR: EMEA was 29% of FY25 revenue, so EUR coupons are partly paid out of EUR income.

Interest rate & sovereign risk · A+

Strong sovereign, rising rates

Low–medium

Spain was upgraded to A+ by S&P. Credit risk is low, but rates are moving up: the ECB hiked to 2.65% and the 10Y yield reached 4.05% as inflation climbed to 4.9%. Locking a fixed coupon now protects against further hikes.

Hedging toolkit · local-currency debt

Instruments used in this project

  • FX forwards (IRP) lock the USD value of every coupon and the principal (§5).
  • FRAs 12×24 and 36×60 lock future EUR borrowing and lending rates (§3).
  • IRS: receive fixed, pay EURIBOR 6M if rates are expected to fall (§6).
  • Cross-currency swap as a single-contract alternative to a forward strip.

Company analysis

Alphabet's business lines, three years of audited financials, its position in Spain and the risks that drive the financing choice.

Business overview

Alphabet reports three segments: Google Services (Search, YouTube, Android, Chrome, devices, subscriptions and Google Play), Google Cloud (infrastructure, Workspace and AI platforms) and Other Bets (Waymo, Verily and others). Advertising across Search and YouTube is still the main revenue driver, and Cloud is the fastest-growing unit: +82% YoY in Q2-26 to $24.8B.

Why Spain? Spain is one of Europe's fastest-growing advertising markets, with ad investment above €11.9B and digital ad growth around 14.7% CAGR, ahead of the UK, Germany, France and Italy. Google runs its European AI hub in Madrid and a Madrid cloud region, and the digital economy is about 26% of Spanish GDP.

Country overview · Spain

Inflation, the euro, the rate structure, political and sovereign risk, and what could change over the next five years.

Inflation dynamics

Spain CPI YoY, %. Dashed line: ECB 2% target. Source: Bloomberg SPIPCYOY; INE (Aug–Sep 2026).

Inflation cooled after the 2022–23 shock but has jumped again to 4.9% in September 2026, the highest since February 2023, driven by fuel and air travel after the Middle East conflict. Core inflation is 3.1%.

Real GDP growth

Annual %, 2026 is a Bloomberg consensus estimate. Sources: INE, World Bank, Bloomberg ECST.

Spain has been one of the euro area's fastest-growing economies since the pandemic. Unemployment fell to 9.87% in Q2-26, down from 11.3% two years earlier.

Exchange rate regime

Free float · euro area

Spain uses the euro, which floats freely against the USD. It is driven by ECB policy, rate differentials and inflation expectations. Market EUR/USD is 1.12 (Oct 20), and analysts see about 1.16–1.17 by end-2026.

The course's Scenario 0 uses 0.90 USD per EUR. All calculations in Sections 3–5 use that rate.

What it means for Alphabet

Base case: Spain keeps growing, but prices and rates stay high and the euro moves little against the dollar.

What could go wrong: higher energy prices from the Middle East conflict, a faster ECB hiking cycle, EU–US and EU–China trade disputes, and volatile equity markets.

Financing takeaway: with rates rising, a fixed-rate EUR bond issued now locks today's cost, and EUR debt matches Alphabet's EUR revenue.

12×24 · borrowing cost. Alphabet buys the FRA (pays fixed, receives floating) to lock 2.60% on EUR it must borrow in year 1–2. If rates rise, the FRA pays out and offsets the higher cost.

36×60 · lending return. Alphabet sells the FRA (receives fixed, pays floating) to lock 2.325% on EUR it will invest in years 3–5.

Both FRA rates are below current spot rates because the Scenario 0 curve is inverted: the market prices lower future rates. An FRA fixes the rate only, not the principal or the EUR/USD rate.

How the hedge works

Alphabet signs ten forwards to buy EUR on each coupon date at the rates above. Whatever happens to spot, it pays a known USD amount, so the forward gain or loss offsets the spot move.

Once hedged, the bond costs 3.66% in USD, almost exactly the US 5-year rate. Under IRP the lower EUR yield is fully offset by the forward premium. The benefit of EUR issuance is the natural hedge and investor diversification, not a cheaper rate.

Why both methods agree, and the trade-offs

Every forward is the spot rate times a ratio of interest factors. Scaling spot by 1/0.9 scales every forward, and so every USD cash flow, by the same 11.11%. Under IRP, Method B collapses to Method A.

  • Forwards: no upfront cost, exact match to coupon dates; but they remove any upside and carry counterparty and rollover needs.
  • Currency options: keep upside if the euro weakens, but cost a premium.
  • Money-market hedge: same result as forwards under IRP, but uses balance sheet.
  • Cross-currency swap: one contract for all ten flows; standard for a 5-year bond.

Interest rate swap strategy

If EUR rates are expected to fall, Alphabet can swap the fixed coupon into floating EURIBOR 6M.

Swap structure

Notional€10,907.22 M
Alphabet receivesFixed 2.528% (5Y par swap)
Alphabet paysEURIBOR 6M
FrequencySemiannual, same dates as coupons
Maturity5 years
Par swap rate from Scenario 0 discount factors: 2 × (1 − DF₅) ÷ Σ DF = 2.528%.

Effective cost of debt

Cost = YTM 2.531% − swap fixed 2.528% + EURIBOR 6M ≈ EURIBOR 6M + 0.3 bp

After the swap the debt is effectively floating. If rates fall, Alphabet's cost falls with them. The trade-off is that it now carries interest rate risk again, and with the ECB currently raising rates, that bet is less attractive today.

Deal recommendation

Side-by-side comparison of the three financing strategies, based on Scenario 0 market data.

StrategyAll-in costKey risks retainedHedge effectiveness
1 · Unhedged fixed EUR bond2.53% in EUR; USD cost unknownFull EUR/USD risk on coupons and principalNone
2 · Fixed bond + FX forwards3.66% in USD, lockedCounterparty risk; no upside if the euro weakensFull
3 · Floating after IRSEURIBOR 6M + ~0 bpInterest rate risk, plus FX risk unless also hedgedPartial

Recommendation: issue the 5-year fixed EUR bond now and hedge it with forwards (Strategy 2). Inflation at 4.9% and an ECB that is hiking argue against swapping to floating. Alphabet's AA+ rating and net cash position let it borrow at near risk-free rates, and part of the EUR exposure is already covered by EUR revenue, so the team could choose to hedge only the share not matched by Spanish and European income.
